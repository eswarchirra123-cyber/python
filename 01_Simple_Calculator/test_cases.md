# Test Cases

|---|---:|---:|:---:|---|
| 1 | 10 | 5 | + | 15.0 |
| 2 | 10 | 5 | - | 5.0 |
| 3 | 10 | 5 | * | 50.0 |
| 4 | 10 | 5 | / | 2.0 |
